02 — Currently Exploring

Three questions I keep coming back to.

Not a list of skills — a list of open problems. Each one has a data pipeline behind it and a reading list I'm still working through.

01 Research

Macroeconomics

Why cycles rhyme but never repeat. I work with growth–inflation surfaces, output gaps, and policy reaction functions — trying to tell a turning point from noise before the revision.

02 Markets

International Fixed Income

Sovereign curves, duration, cross-currency basis. Where a 30bp move in the long end is a policy signal in one country and a funding accident in another — and how to tell which.

03 Systems

AI & Agents

Language models that don't just answer, but act — calling tools, holding state, and returning a number you can audit. The interesting part is the harness, not the prompt.
